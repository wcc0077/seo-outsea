# FN Tech — Industrial RFID Hardware Solutions
# 上海孚恩电子科技有限公司 | Shanghai Fuen Electronic Technology Co., Ltd.

## Company Overview

FN Tech is a professional RFID (Radio Frequency Identification) and IoT (Internet of Things) hardware company established in 2006. Headquartered in Shanghai, China, the company specializes in the R&D, manufacturing, and sales of industrial RFID identification equipment and IoT solutions.

## Core Products

### 1. Smart Mobile Terminals (智能移动终端)
- **Handheld Terminals** — Industrial Android PDAs with barcode/RFID dual-mode scanning
- **Industrial Tablets** — Rugged tablets for field data collection
- **Portable RFID Readers** — Mobile RFID readers for on-site operations

### 2. RFID Readers (RFID读写器)
- **HF RFID Readers** (13.56 MHz) — High-frequency readers for proximity identification
- **UHF RFID Readers** (860-960 MHz) — Ultra-high-frequency readers for long-range reading
- **LF RFID Readers** (125 kHz, 134 kHz) — Low-frequency readers for industrial environments
- **Active RFID Readers** — Long-range active tag readers
- **Protocol Gateway Controllers** — IO-Link, ModbusTCP, Ethernet/IP industrial protocol converters
- **Integrated RFID Products** — Complete RFID reading solutions

### 3. RFID Tags & Labels (RFID电子标签)
- **High Temperature Tags** — Tags rated for extreme heat environments (automotive, manufacturing)
- **Anti-Metal Tags** — On-metal RFID tags with enhanced read performance
- **Fragile Tamper-Proof Tags** — Security tags for anti-counterfeit applications
- **Smart Cards & Sticker Labels** — Paper labels, PVC cards, adhesive tags
- **Active Electronic Tags** — Battery-assisted tags for extended range

### 4. RFID Antennas & Modules
- High-performance RFID antennas for various application scenarios
- RFID reader modules for integration

### Supported RFID Frequencies
- UHF (860-960 MHz)
- HF (13.56 MHz)
- LF (125 kHz, 134 kHz)
- VHF

### Industrial Communication Protocols
IO-Link, ModbusTCP, Ethernet/IP, RS485, RS232
