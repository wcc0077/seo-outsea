# FN Tech — Industrial RFID Hardware Solutions Provider
# 上海孚恩电子科技有限公司

## Company Overview

FN Tech (Shanghai Fuen Electronic Technology Co., Ltd.) is a professional RFID and IoT technology company established in 2006. Located in Shanghai's Caohejing Pujiang Hi-Tech Park, the company specializes in RFID hardware R&D, manufacturing, and IoT solutions.

**Key Facts:**
- Founded: 2006
- Registered Capital: 20.02 million RMB
- Facility: 3,000+ sqm R&D and production base
- Intellectual Property: 70+ patents and certifications
- R&D Team: 60%+ with Master's degrees, 15+ years average industry experience
